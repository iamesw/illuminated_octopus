The BMA is hosting an exhibition title <em>Monet to Matisse: French Moderns, 1850–1950</em> in the new year. These are early design studies for exhibition branding and a visualization of the thought process behind the design. Design direction and design by myself, with additional design by Mary Pat McGlawn.
