"Love a good hobby that can help translate into how you think about work."


It caught me a little off guard. Not quite an offense, but a response that felt completely inaccurate to my purposes. It was in response to my disclosure that I'd used AI to figure out coding issues on my website. And that I've often developed my personal site using platforms that we've used for the Museum website. Maybe it seemed like that is what I was trying to say, using my site as a hobby to better understand the technology. But far from helping think about work, it was to help me think through the processes and learn the technology for the sake of learning a new thing. It's sating a curiosity more than anything. Though I wasn't the type of kid to tear apart my toys to figure out how they work, I have always wanted to understand the things. Perhaps not at a deep level, but enough to have a relatively informed conversation with someone who does.
